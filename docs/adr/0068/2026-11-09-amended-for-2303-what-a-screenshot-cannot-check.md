# Amended for #2303, what a screenshot cannot check, 2026-11-09

The amendment for #2303 has a screenshot paint over what the page draws that shows a text kept
out. This note records, as the rule, what that reading does not cover; nothing else changes.

1. **A text shorter than three characters is not looked for.** Matching it would take ordinary
   words and letters out of every picture.
2. **The match is literal.** As that amendment says, a page that transforms or redraws what it
   shows is outside what a screenshot can check.
3. **Only what the reads around the picture see is painted.** The page is read before and after
   the picture; what it draws and takes away between those reads is not seen.
4. **What moves is painted along a straight box.** An item is painted over the box from where it
   was to where it is (that amendment's item 4), not along the path it took between.
