# Amended for #2303, what a screenshot reads, 2026-11-09

The amendment for #2303 has a screenshot paint over what the page draws that shows a text kept
out. This note records, as the rule, what that reading reads and paints; nothing else changes.

1. **The shortest text looked for is three characters.** Matching anything shorter would take
   ordinary words and letters out of every picture.
2. **The match is literal.** As that amendment says, what is looked for is the text as it was
   kept, in what the page draws.
3. **The page is read before and after the picture.** What either read finds is painted.
4. **An item that moves is painted over the box from where it was to where it is.** The box runs
   from the item's place in the read before the picture to its place in the read after
   (that amendment's item 4).
