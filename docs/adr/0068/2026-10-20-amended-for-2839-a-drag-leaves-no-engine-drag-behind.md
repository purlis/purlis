# Amended for #2839, a drag leaves no engine drag behind, 2026-10-20

1. **A late word of the engine's drag is answered by cancelling it.** A drag of the agent's that
   ends without the engine's word that its own drag started (cut by the person taking over,
   failed, or simply over) leaves a mark of where it ended, on its session. A word that comes
   after that, in the drag's own wait or any time later until the next drag, is answered by a
   `dragCancel` there, carrying what the engine says the drag carries; it is cleanup, made
   whoever drives. A word with no drag of the agent's before it is not acted on, and the next
   drag starts with no mark. *D-2839-1, decided in implementation, to be confirmed.*
