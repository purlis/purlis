# Amended (2026-11-02): Use skill… and the Skills row are on the chat's list, not a tab (#4752)

Records the [charting decisions](https://github.com/purlis/purlis/issues/4576) D9 and
[the pane's chat actions](https://github.com/purlis/purlis/issues/4585) rules 1, 2 and 4, ruled by
the person on 2026-11-02 on the map
[Chats, tabs and panes: a chat isn't a tab](https://github.com/purlis/purlis/issues/4575) (spec
[#4750](https://github.com/purlis/purlis/issues/4750)). This records the decision ahead of the
behaviour, which ships with that milestone's build.

A chat has no tab, and a tab's right-click holds only tab things. A chat's actions are one list,
keyed by the chat, shown the same in the pane head's ⋯, the sidebar row's ⋯ and right-click, and
the palette.

- **Use skill… is a row of that list**, where the 2026-10-21 and 2026-10-30 amendments put it on
  "each chat tab's menu". Its key, `⌘⇧S`, opens it on the focused pane's chat, which is what "in a
  split tab, on the pane that has the keyboard" said.
- **The Skills chip leaves the pane head** and becomes the list's **Skills** row, which opens the
  Skills view on the chat. The pane head keeps one state button and ⋯.
- **Where the budget and the router's timings are shown.** This record's "shown on the chat tab"
  and "the chat tab and the studio show the router's timings", and the 2026-11-01 amendment's
  "the chat's tab", read **the chat's Skills view**, opened from its Skills row or `⌘⇧X`, and the
  studio. The 2026-10-30 amendment already made that view the one answer to what a chat gets.
- **The chip's warning colour goes with the chip.** The needs it warned of stay as the Skills
  view's Needs you cards, reckoned by the same function. Whether the Skills row wears a warning
  mark is the build's to draw; none of the map's rulings says. Where the chip gave way (beside a
  task's breadcrumb, or in a narrow pane), there is no chip to give way.
