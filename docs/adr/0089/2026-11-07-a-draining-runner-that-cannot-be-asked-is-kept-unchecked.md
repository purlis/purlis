# Amended 2026-11-07: a draining runner that cannot be asked is kept unchecked

*Follows the person's ruling of 2026-11-07: a draining runner goes only once it is checked for
work a fetch does not bring, and Let it go in Devices frees one kept for such work. The details
below were decided in implementation, delegated, to be confirmed.* It narrows the 2026-11-02
amendment *a stopped runner's home is kept for its chats' conversations*, where "a home with no
conversation is removed as it is today".

1. **What is kept.** A draining runner that a look would remove with no chat on it, whose home
   holds no chat's conversation, and that cannot be asked what it holds: one that had stopped
   before its drain began and has not run since, and one not yet held whose container the engine
   that made it no longer lists, its home volume still there. Its home may hold a stash, a commit
   on no branch or a clone only there, which the check of a running one finds. So it stays, as one
   kept for such work does, and is not held: the Pools group and Devices say it was not checked.
2. **It is still never woken to be asked** (#2051). One that had stopped before its drain is
   checked by the next look once its container runs again, started by the person, and goes if it
   holds nothing. One whose container is gone is never checked.
3. **Let it go removes it without asking it**: nothing runs on a container stopped or removed, and
   what the person was shown, that it was not checked, is all that is known of it. Work still owed
   a fetch keeps it, as for any runner. A stopped one is removed as a drain removes it; one whose
   container is gone is forgotten as before (#4168).
4. **A runner of which nothing is left goes as before**: its container and home both gone, nothing
   is there to check.
