# A pool that stops asking is said once, and a chat's runs take turns to ask

Amends [ADR 0089](../0089-a-runner-may-be-made-for-minutes-carries-one-role-label-and-serves-charter-over-the-link.md).
Decided 2026-11-01. Each item adds to the opt-in standing as consent, which stays as it was.

1. **One Notice when a Pool goes from asking to minting without asking** (#4442, the person's ruling).
   It says what changed and who controls it, holds nothing back, and is kept until it is dismissed.
   A Pool already minting when the pool is added has none. Off and on again shows it again.
2. **A chat's runs of one tool started together take turns to ask** (#4374, adds to the pacing of the
   amendment of the same date). The turn ends once there is a token, values or a refusal. At most 8 of a
   chat's runs wait at once, and a further one is refused, saying so.
3. **A chat that goes takes its paces with it** (#4374).
