# Amendment, 2026-11-07: the Inbox becomes three views

The amendment ["one list"](2026-10-11-02-one-list.md) is superseded in part, and
["the Inbox heads the right side"](2026-10-11-01-the-inbox-heads-the-right-side.md) amended, by
[ADR 0117](../0117-the-inbox-is-the-persons-own-in-three-views-on-the-right-bar.md). The Inbox is
no longer one view with one list of asks, then Notices, then updates. It is the person's own, in
three views on the right bar: **Asks** and **Updates** (its **Mine** and **Project** lists) at the
top, and **Notices** among the Lower icons, opening in the right half of the Panel. Each list has
its own way to clear it at once, and a plane opened for the first time on a machine counts what
was already there as seen. All doctor findings now stand as one Notice line.

What those amendments say of the ✋ (it counts asks alone, and now opens Asks), of ⌘⇧I (it shows
Asks), of a pane's own Notices, of a chat waiting in another window (now the "In other windows"
group of **Asks**), of the window's own lines, of the retired ✋ list and of system notifications
stands.
