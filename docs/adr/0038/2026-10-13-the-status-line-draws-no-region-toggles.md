# Amendment, 2026-10-13: the status line draws no region toggles

The operator, 2026-10-13: _"bottom toggle icons are redundant now … they can be removed fully"_.
The amendment of 2026-10-10 kept them (_"The status line's toggles stay as each region's way
back"_); that sentence no longer holds. A side is put away and brought back from its own activity
bar (a press of the open view, then of any view), by its key (⌘B, ⌥⌘B) and by the palette's row,
and those are the only ways. The bar stands while its side is away, so the way back is where the
side is. The Panel's halves were never on the status line, and keep their Lower icons and ⌘J.

The status line is still not a region and still cannot be put away. It reads as one status bar:
where you are on the left (the project by its name, with its directory as the tooltip, then the
workspace and its counts), what wants a look on the right (Notices, the doctor, a pin that
drifts), each of those a flat item of the control set. `docs/ui-primitives.md` has the detail.
