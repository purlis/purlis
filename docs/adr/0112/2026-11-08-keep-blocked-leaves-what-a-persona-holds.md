# Amended 2026-11-08: Keep blocked leaves what a persona holds

Decided under the operator's standing order to take the recommended path (D-5617-1, D-5617-5,
D-5617-6, D-5617-7, #5617, from the adversarial reviews of #5595 and #5798), recorded here as a
delegated decision.

**Keep blocked is for one chat, on a vault its persona may not use.** Pressed on a refused vault's
Notice, in the Inbox, or on a run's own notice, it marks the vault kept blocked for that chat at
once, takes back the chat's Run grants on it at once, and answers Don't allow on every ask open on
it. From the mark on, nothing that opens the vault for that chat is given:

- the asks refuse any answer but Don't allow, under the lock the mark is set under, so no Run
  grant is given and no run waiting on the ask is let go on, however far a press had got;
- a press reads the mark as it starts, again once its audit is written, and again before it gives
  the persona the vault, and a press refused after its audit audits what it gave as taken back;
- a refused vault's own Allow reads it again once its audit is written.

**What a persona was given before stays.** An Always allow, or a refused vault's own Allow, that
finished before Keep blocked was pressed wrote a tool line and gave the persona the vault, for
every chat and every teammate. Keep blocked does not take those back, as it never took back what a
persona holds. Once the persona may use the vault, the chat's later runs are no longer runs on a
vault its persona may not use, so Keep blocked no longer holds them. Removing the tool, or the
vault in the Allowed list, takes them back.

**The cost, accepted.**

- An Always allow's tool line written before the mark stays, though the press is then refused and
  its run is not let go on: the line is the persona's, as above.
- A press past its last read of the mark, in the moment between that read and its write with
  nothing slow between them, writes as a press made before it would. The asks still refuse its
  answer.
- Values the person typed into a run's notice are stored before the press is audited, so a press
  refused after that keeps them in the vault, though it opens nothing for the chat.
- Keep blocked's own answers wait for a press of the same chat already under way; the mark and
  the take-back do not. A press in one chat never waits for another chat's.
