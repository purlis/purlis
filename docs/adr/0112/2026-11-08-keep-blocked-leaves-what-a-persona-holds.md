# Amended 2026-11-08: Keep blocked leaves what a persona holds

Decided under the operator's standing order to take the recommended path (D-5617-1, #5617, from
#5595's adversarial review), recorded here as a delegated decision.

**Keep blocked is for one chat, on a vault its persona may not use.** It marks the vault kept
blocked for that chat at once, takes back the chat's Run grants on it at once, and answers Don't
allow on every ask open on it. A press on a run's notice that comes after it gives nothing on that
vault, and one pressed while Keep blocked answers waits for it and finds its ask answered. So
nothing is written or given on the vault once the person has pressed Keep blocked.

**What a persona was given before stays.** An Always allow, or a refused vault's own Allow, that
finished before Keep blocked was pressed wrote a tool line and gave the persona the vault, for
every chat and every teammate. Keep blocked does not take those back, as it never took back what a
persona holds. Once the persona may use the vault, the chat's later runs are no longer runs on a
vault its persona may not use, so Keep blocked no longer holds them. Removing the tool, or the
vault in the Allowed list, takes them back.

**The cost, accepted.** Keep blocked's own answers wait for a press already under way, whose audit
can wait on a stalled session host. The mark and the take-back do not wait.
