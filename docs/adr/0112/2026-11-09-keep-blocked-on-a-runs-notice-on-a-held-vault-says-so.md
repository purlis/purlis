# Amended 2026-11-09: Keep blocked on a run's notice, on a vault the persona holds, says so

Decided under the operator's standing order to take the recommended path (D-5890-1, #5890),
recorded here as a delegated decision. It follows *Keep blocked on a vault the persona holds says
so* (2026-11-09).

A run's own notice offering the vault's own Allow can stay up while its persona comes to hold the
vault: an Allow on the refused vault's Notice, an Always allow in another chat, or the vault's tag.
Keep blocked pressed on it then says what Keep blocked on the refused vault's Notice says:

- **The chat** hears the same line: the person kept the vault blocked, its persona may already use
  it, purlis does not stop its runs on it, and not to use it unless the person says to. It does not
  also hear that it was kept blocked.
- **The person** reads the same sentence. A run's notice is answered from the Inbox, where the
  row it was pressed on goes, so the Inbox says that sentence in a Notice, with the chat it is about,
  above whatever else waits, until the person dismisses it. That holds for Keep blocked on a run's
  notice and on a refused vault alike, and replaces "the Inbox shows no answer's sentence" of the
  amendment before, for these two presses only.
- **One decision per press.** Whether the persona holds the vault is decided once, as the press
  is made, and every run the press answers hears it: a run whose command had stopped waiting is
  nudged with the same line, never "kept blocked", and no chat is told it twice, however many of
  its runs the press answers. That holds for a run asking to allow its program on that vault too:
  its own outcome gives way to the one line, since the press was about the vault.

The run that waited is refused as before, and Keep blocked still takes back nothing the persona
holds.
