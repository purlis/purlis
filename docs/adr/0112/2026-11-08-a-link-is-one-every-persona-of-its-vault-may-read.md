# Amended 2026-11-08: a link is one every persona of its vault may read

The person's ruling on #5745 ("refuse such links"), following
[a picked entry is one its persona may use](2026-11-08-a-picked-entry-is-one-its-persona-may-use.md)
(D-5246-1), which judged a Link press against the asking persona alone and left declared tools as
they were.

A link is written into a vault, and every persona that may use that vault reads through it: by a
Run grant, which the gate already refuses, and by a declared tool, which it does not. Persona A,
which may use both `ops` and `finance`, could link `ops/K` to `finance/X`; persona C, which may
use `ops` and not `finance`, then read `finance/X` through a declared tool that reads `ops/K`.

So a Link press, on a Secret ask's notice or a run's, is refused where some persona that may use
the link's vault may not use the entry's vault, and the refusal names that persona and both
vaults, which the person may see. The personas of a vault are the one the registry tags it for
and each allowed to use it on this machine, as the broker opens it. A registry that can't be read
refuses the press. Both pickers offer only what a press is held to.

The rule is judged at the press, and only there. A link made before this ruling stays as it is,
and so does one made before a persona later came to use its vault: by a tag, an Allow (a refused
vault's, or Always allow's), or a policy lock lifted. That persona's declared tool still reads
through it. Judging those too, as the grant is given or as a declared tool reads, is a ruling of
its own: #5795.
