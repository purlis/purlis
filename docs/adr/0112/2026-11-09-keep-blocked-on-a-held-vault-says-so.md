Decided under the operator's standing order to take the recommended path (D-5801-1 to D-5801-3,
#5801, from the adversarial review of #5798), recorded here as a delegated decision. It follows
*Keep blocked leaves what a persona holds* (2026-11-08).

Keep blocked holds a chat's runs only on a vault its persona may not use. When the persona may
already use the vault, by its tag or by a row of the Allowed list that an Always allow or a
refused vault's Allow made, the press still marks the vault and takes back the chat's Run grants
on it, but it does not say "kept blocked":

- **The chat** hears that the person kept the vault blocked, that its persona may already use it
  (from the Allowed list, or by the vault's tag), that purlis does not stop its runs on it, and
  not to use it unless the person says to.
- **The person**, beside the press on the refused vault's Notice, reads that Keep blocked does not
  stop the chat's runs on the vault, and where to stop them: Settings › Sandbox › Allowed, or the vault's tag,
  and the persona's tool line where one names the vault.

Keep blocked still takes back nothing the persona holds. The Inbox shows no answer's sentence for
any press, Allow included, so its Keep blocked tells the chat the same and the person reads it on
the Notice.
