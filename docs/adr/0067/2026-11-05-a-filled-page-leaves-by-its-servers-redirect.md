# Amended (2026-11-05, #5230; the person's rulings SF-14 and SF-15 on #5096, amending SF-3): a filled page leaves for another site only by its server's redirect

Amends [A filled page holds its browser to its site](2026-11-04-a-filled-page-holds-its-browser-to-its-site.md).

- **A redirect from the approved site's own server, after the form the field was filled in was
  sent there, may take the page to another site.** Links, scripts and the chat's navigate stay
  refused, and the page stays where it was.
- **It continues by itself to a site this browser was on before the fill**, one it went to by an
  address the chat wrote or by the person, a site that answered such a load with a redirect among
  them. Any other site asks the person once, in the sites' ask, before the page goes. Either must
  be in the browser's usual reach and clear of a login held in it.
- **An address that holds the value is refused.**
- **The hold ends when the next page loads**, and the browser goes on from there.
- **A filled form posted straight to another site is refused unless the key is approved for it
  too**: a line of the persona's or the person's Allow for this chat on that origin, over `https`.
  The chat's answer and a page of purlis's own the person sees name the site and say how to approve
  the key. There is no ask per send.
- **An error page in the main frame ends the hold**, in place of what the amendment above says of
  it.
- Browsers on runners behave the same (SF-12). The fill carries the other origins its key is
  approved for (`also`), and a browser's reach the sites the person allowed when asked (`answered`),
  both in protocol 1.74.
