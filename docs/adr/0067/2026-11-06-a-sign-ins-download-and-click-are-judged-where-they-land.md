# Amended (2026-11-06; the steward's decisions on the person's rulings of 2026-11-06): a sign-in's download and click are judged where they land

Amends [A sign-in is held to its own page](2026-11-06-a-sign-in-is-held-to-its-own-page.md).

- **A download is judged on the page it began on** (decided by the steward). The browser's driver
  keeps the page whose frame began each download. While a sign-in is open, one from the form's
  site is brought into the chat's folder only where the sign-in's own page, or a window related
  to it, began it; one another tab began is refused as that tab's reads are. One begun in a frame
  the driver did not know is judged as a page not opened yet is: kept off the site.
- **Input is judged on what is drawn at its point, however deep** (decided by the steward). Where
  the point lands on a frame whose document runs in a process of its own and holds a frame of a
  site the chat is kept off, the driver follows the point into that frame's document and asks
  again, through each such frame. Such a frame drawn other than upright at its own size is not
  followed: input on it is refused.
