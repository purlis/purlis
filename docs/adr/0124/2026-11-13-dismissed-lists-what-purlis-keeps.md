# Amendment, 2026-11-13: Dismissed (N) lists what purlis keeps dismissed (Q234)

Decision 3 ("Dismissed doesn't mean gone", Q183) is narrowed by Q234, from T1-43's review:
**Dismissed (N) lists the Notices whose dismissal purlis keeps** until their cause changes, and
brings any of them back. Those are the families `dismissals.ts` keeps: a dormant workspace, what a
relaunch did to a chat, a doctor finding, a sandbox change that left chats behind, a missing
project, a stale repo and a clone made outside purlis. A Notice of any other family (a sandbox
block, a vault waiting, a report draft) is put away by its own Dismiss for that occurrence, and
shows again by itself the next time it happens, so there is nothing to bring back. The list says
so, above its rows.

A Notice dismissed in the Notices view is counted under Dismissed (N) from the press, while its
Undo stands, and Show again on it is that Undo.

Decision 2 (Q182) stands partly met in phase 1: a control that stands on a Notice links to
itself (today, Trust this clone), and every other core message that names a window control is
held in an exact list in `app/src/windowControls.test.ts`, with its reason, so a new one fails.
Those the window draws in a view or in Settings link to their control in T1-48 (R-357); those a
chat reads belong to decision 1 (Q181).
