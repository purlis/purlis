# Amendment, 2026-11-05 (#5217): a Notices centre card keeps its outline

**In the Notices centre and the window's own lines there, the list of Notices draws no box, and
each Notice is a card of its own.** The rule that an Inbox row draws no edge of its own (amended
2026-10-24) no longer holds for them.

- **The card family's ground** still reaches them, on a plain (`news`) Notice. Trouble and attention
  keep their own grounds.
- **The card family's edge width no longer does.** A card's outline says its kind, and a consent's
  says it asks (ADR 0097 §6). The person ruled on #5222 that a card waiting on consent keeps its full
  waiting edge, and a theme's no-hairline card must not take that away.
- **Any other Notice's outline there is softer**, in the colour of its kind's mark. That edge is
  decoration: the mark and the state's words say the kind, so it isn't held to 3:1.

The list under the title bar and on the page a window with no project shows keeps its box, as before.
