# Amendment, 2026-11-09 (#2994): Allow is held apart from the primary across states

§7 held Allow and danger apart from the primary in each state alone: Allow under the pointer
against the primary under the pointer, at rest against at rest. A person never sees them so. One
control is under the pointer or has the keyboard while the others beside it are at rest, so a
theme could draw Allow under the pointer exactly as the primary at rest and pass.

- **Each look against each look.** Allow and danger in every look they show (at rest, in each
  state but disabled, and in each set of states that hold at once) are measured against the
  primary in every look it shows, by the parts check and by the token floor
  (`Floor::AllowNotPrimary`). Two pairs drawn alike are measured once.
- **The bar.** The token floor keeps its own, at least `ALLOW_FROM_PRIMARY` in every pair. In the
  same state, the parts check keeps §7's: no nearer than the defaults draw that pair. Across
  states it holds each pair no nearer than the family's defaults bring the two in any pair of
  their looks: the nearest the family itself draws them is what it holds as apart. Held to the
  same pair instead, a pair the defaults happen to draw far apart (Allow at rest beside the
  primary under the pointer) would refuse a primary that every look of the family's own stays
  as far from; a primary drawn in the active tab's wash fell back that way.
- **The fallback is the pair's.** A consent variant that breaks it draws its default in its own
  look; where that is not enough, the primary draws its default in the look it was measured in.
  The finding names both (`Allow never takes the primary look: Allow under the pointer beside the
  primary at rest`).
- **A toggle's pressed look is no consent look.** The pressed look is the plain and the ghost
  button's alone (`DRAWN_ONLY`, #3255): the primary, an Allow and danger are never drawn pressed,
  so each keeps its own defaults in every state it is drawn in, and none is measured in a look the
  window never draws.

Every built-in theme holds every pair as it shipped, in every appearance and tint; nothing it
draws changes. An import's tuning counts the floors a theme holds, so a scheme whose tokens bring
a pair together in some workspace tint is tuned apart: Dune's light accent is tuned greyer, as
Allow at rest and the primary under the pointer met in some tints.
