# Amendment, 2026-10-24: the workspace axis is the title bar's (#2916)

The second of this ADR's three strips, the workspaces, is gone from under the title bar: the
project's pinned workspaces are chips in the title bar, after its name, and every workspace is in
the popover its ⌄ opens ([ADR 0054's amendment of 2026-10-24](../0054/2026-10-24-the-workspaces-move-to-the-title-bar.md)).
The axis stands: one workspace is in front, the chats are its chats, and the title bar's chip,
the palette and the switchers are the ways to change it.
