# Amended 2026-11-02: a chat from before the record is kept once it is taken back

**Built by #4683**, on the amendment of the same day, *a chat is found on the pool it ran on*
(#4590). A chat a window started on a pool before that record was kept has none. A restart still
finds it on the pool its workspace runs its chats on, by that amendment's point 1, but kept it
nowhere, so a restart after the setting moved never asked for it again.

**Ruled by the person, 2026-11-02 (D-4683-1):** *a record is added only through the window's own
reattach, never on the runner's word alone.* Their ruling of the same day on #4590 holds as it
stands: the one pool asked is the chat's own recorded pool, and asking it sends no code.

1. **A chat with no record is kept where the window takes it back.** At a restart, a chat with no
   record at all that the pool its workspace runs its chats on says it runs, its runner now or one
   that drains, is kept as that pool's once the window reattaches it there, under the workspace the
   window asked for. That is the record its start would have written: the pool is the setting's,
   and the number and the workspace are the window's. A chat that pool says it runs but that
   nothing takes back is kept nowhere, and a record for another workspace is left as it is.
   *(D-4683-1)*
2. **This widens point 8 of the amendment of 2026-11-02 (#4590).** That point had no line from a
   runner add a record. Now the word of the pool the setting names, that a chat with no record
   runs there, followed by the window's own reattach of it there, adds one. The runner chooses
   none of what is kept: the pool is the setting's, the number and the workspace the window's, and
   its word alone, with no reattach, keeps nothing. A pool the setting does not name is never asked
   for such a chat, so no new pool is asked and none is sent code. *(D-4683-1)*
3. **Rejected: letting these chats age out.** They would end on their own in time, but until they
   did, a setting moved under one left it running on a pool no restart asks, closed by nothing.
   *(D-4683-1)*
