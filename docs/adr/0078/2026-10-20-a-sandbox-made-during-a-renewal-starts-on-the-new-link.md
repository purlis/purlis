# Amended 2026-10-20: a sandbox made during a renewal starts on the new link

**Built by #2624** on the amendment of 2026-10-18 for #2511, under which a chat's sandbox the
window made on a link being renewed, before its chat started, went with that link: it ended
with it, and a start of it on the new link was refused as not started. What follows was decided
in #2624 and is marked *decided in implementation, to be confirmed*.

1. **A sandbox nothing runs in yet is its window's on each of that window's links.** The runner
   names a sandbox a relayed window's link made to every link that named the same window, the
   one a renewal brings up after it included (`take_back` names it to that link too). Any of
   them may act on it and start the window's chat in it. *Decided in implementation, to be
   confirmed* (D-2624-1).
2. **It passes to the window's other link as the link that made it ends.** It is not ended with
   that link while another link of its window lasts; one whose window has no other link, or
   that names no window, still ends with the link that made it, as before (#1999). A sandbox
   whose making link ended before it was made is refused and ended, as before. *Decided in
   implementation, to be confirmed* (D-2624-2).
