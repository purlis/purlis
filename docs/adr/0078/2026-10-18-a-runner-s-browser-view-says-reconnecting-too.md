# Amended 2026-10-18: a runner's browser view says reconnecting too

**Built by #2542**, the part of item 2 of the amendment of 2026-10-18 on the window being told a
runner's link is down that it left for later, under the same ruling of 2026-10-17: *"The window
shows that runner's chats as reconnecting, quietly, and clears it when the link is back."* What is
not quoted from the ruling was decided in #2542 (D-2542-1 to D-2542-3 in the pull request).

1. **A browser view drawn from a runner shows "Reconnecting…" over its last frame** while the
   link to that runner is down: the frame dimmed under the same quiet word as a runner's chat,
   never a Notice. It follows the link the view rides, the window's own, as a chat's pane
   follows its project's.
2. **A view is reconnected** once that link is back, once the view draws again from a snapshot
   (its stream's first frame, which a host sends only while the link is up), or once the window
   can no longer tell (it fell behind its host, or its link to it ended), as a chat is. A view
   closed meanwhile is told nothing more, and one watched again starts reconnected.
3. **The word travels with the view's frames**, on the same channel, so it is never told out of
   order with them. A thumbnail keeps its last frame as it is; the word is said over the view
   the person looks at.
