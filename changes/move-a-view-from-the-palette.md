### Added

- **Move a view from the palette.** Each view but the Inbox has a palette row that moves it to
  its half of the Panel or back to its sidebar, as its icon's menu does. A moved view keeps where
  it was scrolled, and the rows that put a region away name the views that are there now
  (#1752).

- **A half of the Panel whose only views are extension panels no longer contributed** is not
  drawn as an empty half; it comes back with its panels (#1752).
