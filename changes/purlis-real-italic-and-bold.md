### Changed

- **Purlis draws its italic and bold text in real faces.** Comments, emphasis, headings and the
  terminal's bold text use IBM Plex Sans Italic and JetBrains Mono Italic and Bold, bundled with
  purlis, instead of faces the window slants or thickens itself. A project file's theme select
  now offers `purlis` and `purlis-classic`, and a chat's `theme_preview` marks Purlis as a
  preview and can draw its high-contrast dark (#2709).
