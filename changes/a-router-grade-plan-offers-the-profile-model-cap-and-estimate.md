### Added

- **A router's grade plan offers the profile, the model and the cap, and says what it should
  spend.** Grade the router… now lets you pick the profile and the model its chats run on and set
  the cap, as a skill's plan does. It also says what its last grade spent per chat, or that this
  is not known. Where that grade ran on another library, the plan says this one may spend
  otherwise. Pressing Grade consents to the profile, model, cap and dollar limit the plan shows
  (#3618).
