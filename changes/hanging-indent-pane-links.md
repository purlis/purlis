### Fixed

- **A link broken over rows under a reply's or a list's marker opens whole.** Where a harness
  opens a row with a marker such as Claude Code's `⏺ `, a bullet, a list's `- ` or a numbered
  item's `1. `, and indents the rows after it to the marker's words, an address or path it
  breaks at the pane's edge is one link: hovering any row draws all of it, and ⌘-click
  (Ctrl+click) opens the whole target (#7930).
