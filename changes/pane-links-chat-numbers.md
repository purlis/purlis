### Added

- **`chat 641` in a pane is a link.** Its hover says "#641 · <title>", where the chat is and what
  ⌘-click (Ctrl-click off a Mac) does: an open chat comes to the front in its tab or workspace, a
  chat started again is reached under its number now, and a closed or finished one opens its card
  beside the link, with _Read it_ and _Reopen_. A chat's own number in its own pane says "This
  chat". Only the word "chat" and a number link: a bare `#641`, a title, or a number no chat in
  the pane's project had stay plain text (#6217).
