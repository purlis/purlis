### Added

- **A block shows as a marker on its chat: "2 new blocked".** A chat's sidebar row and its
  pane's band count its blocks since you last looked; pressing the marker opens its Chat log
  with Blocks only on. It clears once the chat has been on screen in the focused window or its
  Chat log has been opened. It sends no notification, announces nothing and leaves Quiet and
  snooze alone. Unverified reports never count there: they show a muted dot, and the Chat log
  counts them (R-172, R-174 to R-177, R-299, R-301, R-341; #6421).
