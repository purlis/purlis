### Fixed

- **A chat's merge or enqueue through the forge route is held for you, not refused.** `gh pr merge` (GraphQL `mergePullRequest`) and an enqueue (`enqueuePullRequest`) each raise one Ask that names the pull request and the act, and your press performs that act and no other: a merge merges, an enqueue puts it in the merge queue (by the queue's own method) and never merges it, each only while the head is the one the Ask read. A press made against a row that showed another act is refused as changed. Auto-merge (`gh pr merge --auto`) is refused with a reason, since it would merge any head pushed later, unseen, as is a GitLab accept that sets a merge strategy; the chat is told to ask you to merge it or to enqueue it. Undoing one (dequeue, turn auto-merge off) is never held. Nothing is sent to the forge until you press, and `guards.merge` = `enforce` still needs your press. Where purlis cannot name the pull request, the chat is told why nobody was asked. A push to a branch the chat did not make now says in git's output why no Let this chat push ask was raised (#7543).
