### Changed

- **Rename, Move and Delete a skill in dialogs that say what changes** (Skill studio preview,
  #3685). Rename checks the new name as you type, and lists the personas, skills and pins that
  name the skill and change with it. Move lists each skill level with who gets the skill there,
  and says which chats stop or start getting it for the level you pick. Delete says who uses the
  skill now and that it stays in Deleted for 30 days. Each ends in the result notice with Undo,
  or Bring it back after a delete. A rename now also renames the skill in every workspace's and
  persona's pins. Two labels that meant two things are split: a persona's Remove is now Take
  out of this persona, and a source's Remove is now Remove source.
