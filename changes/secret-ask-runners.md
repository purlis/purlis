### Added

- A chat on a pool's runner that misses a key asks you for it in the window like any other
  chat. The value is stored on your desktop, and the runner gets it only the way a runner chat's
  tool always has: you allow that run, and purlis records it as forwarded. A runner that can't
  receive secrets is told the key was declined and why, and the ask still lets you store the
  key for chats on this machine. A runner purlis couldn't reach to confirm just now is told it
  waits on you instead, and nothing is declined.
