### Changed

- A pool can be forgotten on this machine from every state it cannot be removed from: when its machine does not answer, refuses the sign-in, Docker or forwarding, or presents another key or engine. Before you confirm, the notice lists what may be left on the machine: its runner's container and home volume, the browsers it ran, and the logins given on it.
- The notice for a runner removed outside purlis now reads "Runner gone" and offers "Start again on this pool".
