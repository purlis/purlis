### Changed

- **A pane's notices fold into a bell in its header.** The bell sits in a fixed slot before
  split and close, and counts the pane's notes, in the trouble colour when one is trouble. The
  band of notices is under the header now, so the header never moves when one arrives.
  Minimised, the default, notes fold into the count and asks stay as full cards; expanded shows
  every notice, with Dismiss all, which leaves the asks. The choice is one for every pane and is
  kept across restarts. The cap of two per pane and its "N more in the Inbox" line are gone, and
  an ask has no Dismiss (#4754).
