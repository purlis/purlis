### Changed

- **Notices use the shared buttons and surface.** Every button on a Notice, in a pane, in the
  Inbox or on a Settings page, is now the app's shared small button, and the buttons still wrap
  onto new lines in a narrow pane rather than being cut off. A Notice's box, and what one of its
  buttons opens under it, share one bordered surface with the rest of the app (#1855).
