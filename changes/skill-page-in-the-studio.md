### Changed

- **A skill opens as a page inside the Skill studio** (Skill studio preview, #3682). Choosing a
  skill in the Library opens its page beside the list, or alone with a way back on a narrow tab:
  its name, skill level and state, with Use in this chat, Edit, Grade and a "…" menu (Rename,
  Move to another level, Deprecate, Archive, Open the file, Delete). A banner says why a skill
  isn't plainly on (off, left out, a change to review, an update waiting, hidden by the router)
  and offers the one fix. Its tabs are Overview, Text, History, Grades and Runs, with their
  counts. A built-in skill's page says it can't be edited, moved or turned off. The skill's own
  tab is gone: a run's chip and the Skills view open the skill's page, and a skill tab kept from
  an earlier launch comes back as it.
