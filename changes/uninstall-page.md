### Added

- **How to uninstall purlis.** A new page, *Uninstall purlis* (`docs/uninstall.md`), says where
  purlis keeps its state on a machine and what each step of an uninstall removes: the keyring
  items, what `purlis plugin uninstall` takes back, the app and its command links, and the
  config, data and app folders. It says what an uninstall leaves alone, your projects among
  them, and what to copy out first (#610).

### Changed

- **The README describes purlis as it ships.** Its status is version 0.4 on macOS and Linux, and
  it names the five concepts, the harnesses, the forges and where credentials are kept (#605).
