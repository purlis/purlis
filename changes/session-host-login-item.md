### Added

- **purlis keeps running in the background while something needs it.** Once you have a
  trigger or a paired device, Quit leaves your chats running: the window closes, and the menu
  bar says why purlis is still running, with *Open purlis* and *Stop purlis and end every
  chat*. purlis starts again in the background when you log in, and starts its chats' host
  again if it stops unexpectedly. Where your desktop shows no tray, the window stays open and
  offers Stop instead. Neither triggers nor paired devices are in purlis yet, so for now
  nothing changes, and Quit ends every chat as before (FD-17, #655).
