### Security

- **A screenshot covers more of what a browser kept out.** A code typed across boxes is found
  with dots, slashes or bullets between its characters, and stays kept once a field beside the
  boxes adds to it. Two kept texts side by side across an element's edge are each painted over,
  and so is an SVG `use` that may draw a kept text. A screenshot is withheld when the page draws
  a backdrop through a filter of its own, casts a text's shadow past what is painted, or draws
  text that shows a kept text through a filter that may draw it elsewhere.
