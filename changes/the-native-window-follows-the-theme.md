### Added

- **The window's native parts follow the theme** (#2328). The traffic lights, the menus and the
  dialogs are drawn light for a light theme and dark for a dark one, whatever the system's own
  appearance, and the window opens in the theme's background colour, with no flash of another.
- **Forced colours and more contrast** (#2328). With a contrast theme that forces its colours,
  focus, the tab you are on, the row the keyboard is on and each chat's state mark are drawn in
  the system's colours and with a shape. When the system asks for more contrast, a theme's
  appearance tagged high contrast is drawn for you, and drawn again as soon as the setting
  changes.
