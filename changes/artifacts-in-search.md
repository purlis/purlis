### Added

- **Find a pinned artifact by what its page says.** Typing in the command palette lists the
  pinned artifacts of the project in front whose pages say it, after the actions, and Enter
  opens the artifact's tab; `purlis artifact search <words>` finds them on the command line.
  Only a pinned folder's own pages are read, through the same gate as its tab, never a link out
  of it and never anyone's data files (ADR 0120, #5968).
