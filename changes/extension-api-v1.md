### Changed

- **Three Skill studio capabilities are now version 1 of the extension API.** `grader`,
  `studio-panel` and `skill-router` no longer read as experimental in the approval prompt or the
  Extensions list, and [`docs/extension-api.md`](docs/extension-api.md) records their messages.
  `skill-source`, `skill-lint` and `skill-template` stay experimental (#2786).
