### Fixed

- A failed clone's notice goes once its repo lands, from its row, its chip or Settings. A failure that named several repos keeps only the ones still not cloned.
- A new workspace's repo whose clone failed keeps its row in the Repos and Changes views, with the reason and Retry, and Dismiss puts it away.
- Explorer no longer says "No repos in this workspace" above the repos it lists as not cloned here.
- A repo that landed while its workspace was not focused, and was dropped before it was, no longer stays as a "Cloned" row.
