### Added

- **An unused browser stops, and a device runs a budget of browsers.** A chat's browser that
  neither the chat nor you used for ten minutes stops; the chat's next browser call says so and
  opens a new one. A browser you drive, or one waiting for you, never stops this way. The
  device runs at most a budget of browsers from its memory (16 GB: 4, 32 GB: 8, 64 GB: 16),
  beside the three a project's chats hold, and a browser past either is refused in words that
  say which limit it met (#2023).
