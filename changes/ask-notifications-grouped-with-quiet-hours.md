### Added

- **Quiet hours for notifications.** Settings › You › Notifications has quiet hours, off until
  you turn them on, with a start and an end time (22:00 to 07:00 unless you pick others). In
  them purlis shows no system notification; what chats ask still waits in the Inbox. The same
  page says whether your system lets purlis show notifications, and asks for that only when you
  press Allow.

### Changed

- **Several chats asking together are one notification.** "3 chats need you" names them, and
  clicking it opens the Inbox at the first one still waiting. A notification is still only for
  something a chat asks you, never for an update.
