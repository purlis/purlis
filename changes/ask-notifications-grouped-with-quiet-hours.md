### Added

- **Quiet hours for notifications.** Settings › You › Notifications has quiet hours, off until
  you turn them on, with a start and an end time (22:00 to 07:00 unless you pick others). In
  them purlis shows no system notification and what chats ask waits in the Inbox; when they
  end, one notification names the chats still waiting on you. Whether purlis may show
  notifications at all is up to your system's notification settings.

### Changed

- **Several chats asking together are one notification.** "3 chats need you" names them, and
  clicking it opens the Inbox at the first one still waiting. A chat that asks while that
  notification is still fresh is named in one more a few seconds later, if it still waits. A
  notification is still only for something a chat asks you, never for an update.
