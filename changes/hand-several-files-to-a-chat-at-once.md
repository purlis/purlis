### Added

- **Hand several files to a chat at once.** In the explorer's *Files*, pick rows with Cmd or Ctrl
  and a click, a range with Shift and a click, or from the keyboard with Shift and the arrows.
  *Add to a chat's context* under the tree types them all into the chat you pick as one paste,
  never sent. Esc or *Clear* lets the picks go. The one-row *Add to a chat's context* on a row's
  menu works as before (#1151).
