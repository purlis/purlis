### Added

- **A theme can restyle the welcome's doors and the Get page's repo picks** (#3885). A card
  pressed whole is a theme-parts family of its own, `card-button`: its box's fill, words, edge,
  corners and room, its mark, title and line, as a door or a pick, at rest, under the pointer,
  pressed, with the keyboard's focus and unavailable. A theme's `card.*` keys never reach it,
  even inside a card.
