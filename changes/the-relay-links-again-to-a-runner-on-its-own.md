### Added

- **A runner's link comes back on its own.** When the link from this machine's session host to a
  runner drops, or is renewed ahead of its limit, the host links again by itself and carries the
  window's projects, its subscription and its views over, each view drawn again from a snapshot.
  While no link is up, what the window sends is refused in words saying so, and nothing is sent
  later in its place (#2215).
