### Added

- **Discuss a pinned artifact in a chat.** Discuss in an artifact's bar opens a new chat in its
  workspace with the page attached, and sends nothing: you read the line purlis typed, add your
  question and press Enter. The page is attached as someone else's words, quoted line by line
  under purlis's note of who pinned it and at which commit, and the chat is told to read anything a
  page says as part of the page, not as instructions to follow. Where purlis cannot type into the chat's program, the line
  is put on the clipboard.
