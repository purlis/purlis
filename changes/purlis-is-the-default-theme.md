### Changed

- **Purlis is the default theme.** A new install, and any project, workspace or machine where
  nobody chose a theme, now draws Purlis: the lavender dark from the designer's foundation, in
  IBM Plex Sans and JetBrains Mono with Lucide's icons, in its high-contrast dark when your
  system asks for more contrast. Purlis Classic, the green look purlis had until now, is the
  alternative: choose it in Settings › Theme. A theme you or your project already chose is kept
  as it is, and so is a pick of `purlis-classic`, `purlis-dark`, `purlis-light` or `system`.
  Purlis is no longer marked Preview, and a new theme draft extends Purlis (#3034).
