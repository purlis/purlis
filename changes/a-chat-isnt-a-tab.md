### Changed

- **A chat isn't a tab.** Every chat lives in the sidebar, and a tab is a layout of panes you
  made. The first launch after this update keeps the tab that was in front and every tab with a
  split, a view or a pin. Each other tab of a single chat closes, and its chat keeps running in
  the sidebar. A workspace left with no tab shows its first chat. The Chats view's This tab
  scope is gone: Workspace and All stay, and a view that was on This tab shows Workspace
  (#4758).
