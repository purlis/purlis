### Changed

- **The close that ends a chat keeps its danger cue under any theme** (#3295). A theme can draw
  the quiet buttons' hover its own way, which used to replace the red look of the pane close
  that ends a chat. Under the pointer, its × now always takes the danger colour and a thin edge
  of it, drawn after a theme's own rules, so ending a chat never looks like any other quiet
  hover.
