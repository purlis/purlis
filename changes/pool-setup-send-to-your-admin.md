### Added

- **Pool setup: Send to your admin.** Where Check the cluster finds what you can't add with your
  own sign-in, Send to your admin gives words to copy for your cluster's admin: what the check
  found, what purlis needs, the guide and the commands. They hold no password, token, key or
  credential. The step then shows "Waiting for your admin" across a close, checks again by itself
  (every half minute while the tab is open, every ten minutes while it's closed), and gives one
  entry in Needs you once the cluster is ready. `purlis pool setup` prints the same words.
