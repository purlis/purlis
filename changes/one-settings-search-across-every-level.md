### Added

- **One settings search across every level.** The box in Settings' side column now searches every
  setting at You, the project and the workspace at once. What it finds is the first page of the
  nav, "All results", under the level and page each setting is on. A setting at the level you
  are on is changed right there; one at another level has a Go to button that opens that level
  with the keyboard in its field. Set here, beside the box, narrows to the values set at the
  level you are on. Every setting is also a row of the Command palette, such as "Project
  settings: Saving › Mode", which lands on its field.
