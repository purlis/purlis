### Changed

- **purlis has its own look: a calm teal on warm greys.** Both built-in themes are drawn in
  Verdigris, a slightly desaturated teal accent on warm graphite (dark) and warm paper (light)
  neutrals, easy on the eyes in long sessions. Every text and mark still clears its contrast
  floor in both themes and in every workspace colour, and the status colours did not move. The
  themes are now called `purlis-dark` and `purlis-light`; a project, a workspace or a saved choice
  that names a theme by its old id keeps drawing the same theme under the new name (#1838).
