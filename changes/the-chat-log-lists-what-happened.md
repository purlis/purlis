### Added

- **A chat's Chat log now lists what happened to it between its start and its end**: each time
  its sandbox was lifted for a run or put back on, and who did it; each ask, with its answer and
  who answered, on one row; each action purlis held for you, such as a merge only your Merge
  merges; and each time it was paused for going round and round. Its allowed calls are one line
  above the rows ("212 allowed · 9 by your rules"), and a chat whose reports purlis cannot check,
  or that ran with No sandbox, says so first. The event log now records an ask being raised, and
  who answered each ask; a loop or budget pause, and your answer to it, are written as they
  happen, as is your answer to a harness's permission prompt from the window.
