### Added

- **A locked keychain at a chat's start asks you to unlock it.** A chat on a model key reads the
  key from your keys once, when it starts, and a locked keychain is an Ask, *Your keychain is
  locked. Unlock it to start this chat.*, with *Unlock*: in place in New chat, or in Needs you
  when the chat is unattended. The chat starts once the keychain unlocks, with no second press
  (T1a-61, #6763).
