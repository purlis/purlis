### Added

- **Place a pane with the keys or single presses, wherever a drag could put it.** Press a pane's
  grip, the first control in its head, or press Enter or Space on it, or ⌘⇧M (Ctrl+Shift+M off a
  Mac), to pick the pane up. ⌘⇧M on a chat's row in the sidebar or on a tab picks that up
  instead, and so does a chat row's Place… under the pointer. Every other pane then shows five
  targets, and the strip a New tab. Arrow keys choose a side, Tab goes to the next pane, Enter
  drops and Esc puts it back. Each target says what dropping there would do, or why it can't,
  and the result is drawn before the drop. A screen reader hears the pick-up, each target, the
  drop and a refusal. The drops follow the same rules as a drag, with the same Undo (#4764).
