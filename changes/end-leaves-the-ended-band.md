### Changed

- **Ending a chat leaves its pane on the ended band.** After End…, or a Smart close once its
  record is written, the chat's last screen stays, read-only, under the ended band, and the
  keyboard stays where it was. A Smart close's band has Resume, which starts the chat again from
  its record in the same pane. Clear pane now closes the pane, and the strip's last tab keeps one
  empty pane (#5019).
