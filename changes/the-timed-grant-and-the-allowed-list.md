### Added

- **The host side of "Allow for this chat, for 15 minutes or an hour."** The commands behind a timed
  grant and the Allowed list are in: what an ask offers, giving a timed grant with a one-line
  reason, and the list of what was allowed with its reason, countdown and Remove, as a list
  component that is not on a page yet. The grant is the chat's alone (a chat it dispatches does not
  get it), is given only in your window, ends at its time, when the chat closes, when its rule is
  removed or when you remove it, and leaves one quiet line in the Journal each time. A rule's
  `@timed` and an organisation's or project's `timed` lock cap it or switch it off, and an ask whose
  `@keep` is `once` offers none. The ask's buttons and the page for the list follow (#7030).
