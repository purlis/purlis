### Added

- **Settings › Theme shows the component parts a theme sets.** Each card counts the parts its
  theme sets. A new Parts row names the families the chosen theme sets, each with the parts
  manifest's description of it. Under Show each part is every family and part purlis has, with
  the states each draws and the version it came in. Under a part are the keys the theme sets,
  each with its value and whether the theme or one of its options sets it. A filter finds a part
  by its name, its description, a key or a value (#2947).
