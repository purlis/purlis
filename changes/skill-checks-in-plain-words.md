### Added

- **A skill's checks, in plain words, and one chat to write them** (Skill studio preview). A
  skill's Grades tab speaks of **checks** of two kinds: **Does it get picked?** (its trigger
  tests) and **Does it do the job?** (its outcome evals), each a card with its own results with
  the skill and without it, and its technical name in a small label. Once graded, one sentence
  says how the skill did. A skill with no checks offers **Ask a chat to write checks**, which opens
  a chat with its request typed and not sent; both kinds come back in one draft that waits on the
  tab to be accepted or discarded whole. Write them yourself starts the skill's
  `evals/triggers.json` from a short template and opens it to write in (#3925), and a kind with no
  checks offers a chat to write just those.
