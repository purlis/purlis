### Added

- **Purlis light and Purlis high contrast are ready to use.** Choose either as Purlis's appearance
  in Settings, or have Purlis follow the system: it draws light on a light system and high contrast
  when the system asks for more contrast. With no choice made, Purlis stays dark (#2924).

### Fixed

- **Purlis light's warning, danger and diff washes now show their colour.** The amber behind a
  waiting count or a pane's warning, the red behind a danger notice, and the diff's red and green
  were drawn as a dark hue laid thin over a light background, so they read as grey. Each one is
  now its hue's purest colour, at the same strength (#2924).
