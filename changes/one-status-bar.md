### Changed

- **The bar at the bottom of the window reads as one status bar.** Notices and Doctor are flat
  items on it, each with its icon, its word and its count, in place of two boxed buttons. Where
  you are is on the left (the project, the workspace and its counts) and what wants a look is on
  the right (Notices, Doctor, a pin that drifts).

- **The project is shown by its name.** The bar shows the last part of the project's directory
  and no longer the whole path; rest the pointer on it, or reach it with Tab, to read the
  directory.

### Removed

- **The two icons at the bottom left that put a side away.** They did what each side's own
  strip of icons already does: press the open view to put the side away, and any view to bring
  it back. ⌘B and ⌥⌘B (Ctrl+B and Ctrl+Alt+B off a Mac) and the palette's rows do the same.
