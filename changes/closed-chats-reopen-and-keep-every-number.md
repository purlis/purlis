### Added

- **Reopen a chat you started yourself after it closed.** *Reopen* on its card starts it again on
  its conversation, where its harness named one; where it cannot, the card says why. A handoff's
  card reopens it the same way.
- **Find a closed chat in the Chats list.** Type its number, a number it had before a restart, or
  words of its title in the list's filter, and it is listed under *Closed chats*; choose it to
  open its card.

### Fixed

- **Chats dropped at Start fresh, or left out as a launch put chats back, keep their card.** Their
  number used to say "no chat".
- **A chat's older numbers still find it after a restart.** A `chat 641` link printed before the
  chat was restarted under a new number opens that chat, or its card once it has closed.
