### Added

- **Purlis has a light high-contrast appearance.** Choose "light-high-contrast" as Purlis's
  appearance in Settings, or let Purlis follow the system: on a light system that asks for more
  contrast, Purlis now draws light high contrast instead of standard light. Its text, focus ring,
  borders and marks are at least as strong as in Purlis's dark high contrast (#4677).
