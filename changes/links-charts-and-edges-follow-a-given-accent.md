### Fixed

- **Links, the first chart colour and an imported theme's selected edge follow a given
  accent.** An accent you give over Purlis now turns the link and the first chart colour too,
  wherever they can stay apart from the state colours. A teal accent keeps Purlis's link,
  because a teal link would read as success. What a theme draws as its accent (the focus ring,
  the selected row's edge, the first chart colour) is now the accent you give, so a base16
  import's selected edge matches its ring. Over Purlis Classic, the primary's fill follows its
  surface when an accent turns it (#5157).
