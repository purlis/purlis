### Added

- **Publish a skill, or export it** (Skill studio preview, #2781). `purlis skill publish` opens a draft pull or merge request on a skill source with the skill, the skills it refers to and a summary of their grades, after you review everything that would go and say yes at a terminal. `purlis skill export` writes a folder or zip that works outside purlis. Anything that looks like a secret stops both.
