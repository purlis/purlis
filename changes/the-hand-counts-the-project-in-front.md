### Changed

- **The ✋ counts only the project in front.** Its number no longer adds "+N elsewhere" for the
  other open projects: each project's tab on the rail shows its own count, and the Dock badge
  still shows the total of every open project in every window. Pressing the ✋ opens the Asks of
  the project in front and brings no other project forward. Notifications, Quiet and the
  Expiring sound still cover every project, and clicking a notification still brings its project
  forward with its ask open.
