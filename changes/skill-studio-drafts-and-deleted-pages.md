### Changed

- **Drafts and Deleted in the Skill studio are pages of their own** (Skill studio preview,
  #3690, #3691). Drafts lists each skill a chat drafted. Pick one to see what it writes, file by
  file, then Try it on a program, Accept it or Discard it. A draft whose project copy changed
  since says so and offers to ask the chat to redo it, in place of Accept. Deleted lists the
  skills deleted in the last 30 days with the days left to bring each back. Pick one to Bring it
  back, with its history and grades, or to read its last text.
