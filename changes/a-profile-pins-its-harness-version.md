### Added

- **A profile pins its harness version.** A profile's first chat pins the version of its harness
  installed, and its chats then run, and its conversations resume, on that version. A profile
  you made follows its harness's minor and patch updates, and the chat's tab says so; on another
  major version a chat does not start, and Update the pin in Settings › You › Profiles moves
  the profile to it. A profile adopted from the harness's own folder follows the harness, and
  the chat's tab says when its version moved (#2038).
