### Changed

- **Every select and every box with suggestions is one family** (#2906). Settings' choices, the
  filters of Dispatches and the Skill studio, Search's scope, the browser bar's page size and
  Add a skill are drawn and walked the same way: a drawn chevron and a quiet placeholder, the
  list under the box on the menus' surface with the menus' rows, the arrows stopping at their
  ends, Escape leaving the value as it was, and a long list (ten options or more) opening with a
  filter that takes what you type. Themes can now style a select's box and a combobox's part by
  part.
