### Changed

- **Every table is one family** (#2911). The Dispatches tab, a workspace's repos in Saving, a
  skill's runs, totals and history, the Skill studio's tables and a vault's secrets are walked
  the same way: one Tab stop, the arrows along the rows and across the cells into a cell's
  button, and a row's menu under the ⋯ at its end, on a right-click or Shift+F10. Each table
  stands in a rounded card that scrolls it sideways where it is narrow, its column names in small
  capitals. Settings' tables of fields keep their own keys and still stack into a form where
  Settings is narrow. Themes can now style a table part by part.
