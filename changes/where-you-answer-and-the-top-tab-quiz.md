### Added

- **A task's question that reaches you is a quiz at the top of its session's pane.** It says who
  asks, why it reached you (its asking chat passed it on, didn't reply in 100 seconds, or has
  closed), the asking chat's note as its own, and how long it has waited, with a box to answer
  in. One at a time, oldest first, with a count of the rest. It never takes the keyboard or
  switches a tab, and it stays in the ✋ and the Inbox wherever it's drawn (#4565).
- **Where you answer**, in Settings › Chats list: from the top (the default) or from each
  session, where each question waits in its task's own tab. It's offered once, as a quiet line
  above the first question, and a change applies to open questions at once. Any task can still
  be opened and answered either way (#4565).
