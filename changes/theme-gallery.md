### Added

- **Settings › Theme is a gallery.** Each theme you can choose shows as a card with a picture of
  it in each of its appearances; pointing at a card (or moving to it with the arrow keys) draws
  it in the window, and leaving puts yours back. Pressing a card makes it your choice at that
  level, with a badge saying where what is drawn comes from. Under the cards are the chosen
  theme's appearances and options, what the theme check found in it, **Duplicate as my theme**
  (a theme folder of your own that extends it) and **Open folder**. At the You level it is the
  machine default, which had no picker before (#2331).
