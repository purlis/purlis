### Added

- **The Skill studio's Whose chats picker lists the project's personas.** Under *As a persona*,
  each persona is listed as *A chat as steward*, with its role under its name, so you can see a
  persona's skills without going through its page first. Pick one and *In which workspace*
  offers the project's root and each workspace the persona was dispatched into, with how many of
  its chats ran there. That shows the skills a chat dispatched there gets: the workspace's and
  the persona's together. Behind the `skill-studio` flag, like the rest of the studio (#2935).
