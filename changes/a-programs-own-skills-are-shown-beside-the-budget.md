### Added

- **A chat's skill budget shows its program's own skills beside it** (Skill studio preview,
  #3324). The Skills view's line under a chat's set now adds what its program lists of its own
  skills, from your folders and its plugins, such as `Codex's own, not counted: 2 skills, 1,310
  characters`. purlis's budget never counts them: the program lists them against its own limit.
  The studio's Routing page says what the budget counts, and `purlis skill list` shows each
  program's own figure.
