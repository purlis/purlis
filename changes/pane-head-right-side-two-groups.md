### Changed

- **A pane head's buttons are in two groups.** The chat's actions come first, in this order:
  the state button, the bell, then ⋯. After a thin divider come the pane's controls: Split right,
  Split down, then Close in the corner. Tab moves through them in the order they're drawn. A pane
  showing a task, which has no Close, keeps the same groups. So does a view's pane, which has
  only the pane's controls (#5203).
