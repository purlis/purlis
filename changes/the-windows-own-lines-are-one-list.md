### Changed

- **A window with no project lists what it says in one place.** A slow start, a launch without
  the session bus, vaults left waiting, a project that has moved or gone, and the opener's own
  lines now form one list at the top of the opener's page, the most important first (#1688).

- **An alert's fix says what it acts on.** The Inbox's alert rows press "Update workspace
  layout" rather than "Reinit", and "Use the built-in theme…" rather than "Use built-in…". The
  questions about moving a file aside offer Keep it first (#1719).

- **A screen reader hears the lines that say something is being read.** Examples are "Reading
  the vaults…", "Finding files…", and a list purlis could not read (#1719).
