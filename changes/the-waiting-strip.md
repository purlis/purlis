### Added

- **A chat waiting on you says what it waits for at the top of its pane, however much it writes
  after.** A permission or a question shows in its own words, which no later turn replaces. A
  chat whose turn ended on you shows its last message, with the few before it a press away, so a
  question a later turn pushed aside is not lost. Show all opens the whole text. The strip never
  takes the keyboard, and goes once you answer (#4563).

### Changed

- **A long last message keeps its end.** Past 4 KiB, a chat's last message is cut in its middle,
  so a question on its last line still shows in the Inbox, the strip, and a report purlis makes
  from a task's last message (#4563).
