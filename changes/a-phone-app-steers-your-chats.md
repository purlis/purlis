### Added

- **A phone app steers your chats.** `mobile/` is purlis for an iPhone: pair it once with a
  desktop by scanning the code in Settings › You › Devices, and Home lists what needs you across
  every paired desktop, then each desktop's chats by workspace. A chat opens as its timeline
  over a mirror of its terminal at the desktop's size, with quick keys, a composer whose Send is
  a prompt, Stop, and an ask sheet that offers only the choices that do not last. A shell tab
  gets the mirror and the keys. The app locks with Face ID on every open and after five minutes
  away, reconnects on its own when it comes back, and while a desktop is away its controls are
  disabled: nothing is sent late.
