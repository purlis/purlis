### Added

- **Web addresses in a pane are links.** In a chat's, a shell's or a one-job program's pane, and
  in a saved chat's read-only terminal, hovering an http or https address underlines it and shows
  where it goes. ⌘-click (Ctrl-click off a Mac) opens it in your browser; a plain click still
  selects text. purlis opens no other scheme, and says why when it won't open one (#6215).
