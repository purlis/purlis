### Added

- **Find a chat by the number other chats call it.** A chat tab's tooltip starts with the chat's
  number, `#641 · steward 4`, and its pane's header shows `#641` beside its name. A chat started
  again shows its new number. Typing `641` or `#641` in the command palette finds that chat in
  any workspace of the project, even one with no tab in this window, ahead of anything whose
  name only contains those digits. The Chats list's filter finds a chat by its number too (#6212,
  #6213).
