### Added

- **Pick a project from your repos.** Signed in to GitHub or GitLab in purlis, the welcome's
  **Get a project from your forge** page lists the repos you and your organisations or groups
  can reach, on every account you signed in to, with purlis projects first and marked. Search
  it by name or owner, and press a repo to clone it and open it the same way a pasted URL is.
  A forge you have not signed in to says so, with **Sign in…** on the page itself, and the URL
  box is always there. purlis finds a project by reading the top-level file names of up to 50
  repos per account, inside its request budget, and clones nothing until you pick (#1818).
