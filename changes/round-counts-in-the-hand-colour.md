### Changed

- **Every count of what waits on you is a small round badge in the ✋'s colour.** The ✋, each
  project's tab on the rail and the Asks chips draw one character: 1 to 9, then 9+, so a badge
  never changes shape or crowds its icon; a screen reader still hears the real number. A count
  that changes slides once, and under reduced motion it just changes. The rail's tab counts, once
  amber, are now in the ✋'s colour too.
