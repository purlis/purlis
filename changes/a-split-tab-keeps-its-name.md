### Changed

- **A split tab keeps its first chat's name and shows the pane that needs you.** Splitting a tab
  or moving between its panes no longer renames it. When its first chat closes, it takes the next
  one's name. Its mark is its most urgent pane's, so a pane that needs you shows on the tab while
  another pane is focused, and a task shown in a pane counts too (#5342).
