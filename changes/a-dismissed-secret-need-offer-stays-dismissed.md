### Fixed

- **A Secret need offer you dismiss stays dismissed.** After a first fill of a key a persona
  does not declare, purlis offers to add it as a Secret need; dismissing that offer used to bring
  it back on the next fill. Now that persona and key are not offered again on this machine until
  the persona's Secret needs change, and another key or persona still is. To have it offered
  again, open the key's row menu in its vault and choose **Offer as a Secret need ▸** and the
  persona. Only the names are kept (#5159).
