### Added

- **A Panel under both sidebars and the editor, with Repos in its left half.** The left activity
  bar runs the window's whole height, and its Lower icons, below a divider, switch the Panel's
  left half. Repos and branches is a view of its own there, out of Explorer, which keeps
  Workspaces and Files; picking a repo or branch in Repos still chooses where the next chat
  starts and what Files lists. A press of the open Lower icon puts the half away, and with both
  halves away the editor has the whole height. ⌘J (Ctrl+J elsewhere) hides and shows the Panel,
  and so do _Hide the panel_ and _Show the panel_ in the palette. Drag its handle to resize it
  (30% of the window by default, 120px to 70%), and double-click the handle to put it back. Its
  height and whether it is away are kept for each project; after the update it opens once
  (spec #1736, #1737).
