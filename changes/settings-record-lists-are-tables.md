### Changed

- **Settings' lists of what this machine keeps are tables.** You › This machine's recent
  projects, pins and approvals, your forge accounts, Signed commits' accounts and your other
  devices' keys, this machine's profiles and your pools are each a table, as Your hosts is: a
  row a record, its name first, and its actions in one place at the end of every row. A pool's
  profiles are a list inside its row, and a profile's sign-in opens under that list (#6301).

### Fixed

- **A profile's Import again and Sign in are named by the words they show**, so voice control
  and a screen reader find them by what is on screen (#6301).
