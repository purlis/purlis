### Changed

- **Every select is the same select.** Settings, dialogs and the Dispatches and Search filters
  open one list in the window's menu style, with a tick on the current choice. Enter or an arrow
  opens it, and typing a letter jumps to an option (#1860).
- **On/off settings are switches.** The Chats list settings under You, and an extension's on/off
  in Extensions, turn on and off at once. Options in a form, such as Start a chat's, stay
  checkboxes and are applied by the form's button (#1860).
