### Changed

- **⌘T opens an empty tab and ⌘N starts a new chat**, so the keys say what they do (Ctrl+T and
  Ctrl+N off a Mac, where a chat's terminal keeps them for its shell). ⌘⇧T is still New shell. If
  your fingers know ⌘T as a new chat, it now gives you an empty tab that asks what goes there
  (#5292).
- **An empty pane asks what goes here.** It offers New chat and New shell, and says you can drag a
  chat in from the list. A tab with nothing in it is called Empty tab until it holds something
  (#5292).

### Added

- **The strip's `+` has a list.** Pressing `+` still opens an empty tab. Rest the pointer on it, or
  press Down on it, to choose what else opens in a new tab: New chat, New shell, a browser,
  Settings and the other views, each with its key. A long list has a filter (#5292).
