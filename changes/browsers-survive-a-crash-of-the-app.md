### Added

- **Browsers survive a crash of the app.** A browser a chat used is back under its chat, with the
  name the chat knew it by, when purlis comes back within the grace period, and its view draws
  again from the next frame. A browser you were driving is still yours, a pinned one whose chat
  did not come back stays under Pinned browsers, and one you opened yourself comes back in a tab
  of its own. A browser nothing takes back is ended (#2025).
