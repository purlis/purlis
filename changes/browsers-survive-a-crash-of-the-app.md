### Added

- **Browsers survive a crash of the app.** A browser a chat used is back under its chat, with the
  name the chat knew it by, when purlis comes back within the grace period, and its view draws
  again at once. A browser you were driving is still yours. One you opened yourself, and any you
  had open in a tab of its own, comes back in a tab. A pinned one whose chat did not come back
  stays under Pinned browsers. A browser nothing takes back is ended, and one signed in with a
  login goes back to its chat only if that chat may still use the login (#2025).
