### Changed

- **The Chats view scrolls as one piece, and each row leads with its persona** (#3342). The
  title with New chat, the filter, the scope and the chips stay at the top on the panel's own
  fill while the rows scroll. Each workspace's name stays under them, and rows are 30px.
  Every chat's row starts with the mark of the persona it runs as, in its colour. The chat's
  state is a small dot on the mark's corner, and a done chat shows none. The tooltip and the
  screen reader say both, for example "steward, needs you".
