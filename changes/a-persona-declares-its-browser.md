### Added

- **A persona declares its browser, and Always for this persona.** A persona's sandbox section
  may say `browser = "project"` or `"open-web"`: the answer its chats' browser Ask has
  preselected, which you still confirm. The Ask also offers **Always for this persona**, kept on
  this machine for every chat opened as that persona in the project, listed and removable in
  Settings › Sandbox; removing it ends the browsers it opened. An administrator's policy without
  the `you` scope forbids it (#2241).
