### Added

- **A persona declares its browser, and Always for this persona.** A persona's sandbox section
  may say `browser = "project"` or `"open-web"`: its chats' browser Ask marks that answer "uses
  this", in its place, and you still answer it. The Ask also offers **Always for this persona**,
  kept on this machine for every chat opened as that persona in the project, listed and
  removable in Settings › Sandbox; removing it ends the browsers it opened. An administrator's
  policy without the `you` scope forbids it (#2241).
