### Added

- **A narrow window folds panes to rails.** When a split can't give each side about 40 columns
  by 8 rows, the side without the focus folds to a rail beside the other, or a bar above or below
  it. Each folded pane shows its icon, its notices count and ✋ while it asks, and a press opens
  it. The layout comes back when there's room, and the sidebar and the Explorer stay where they
  were. Split right and Split down grey out with their reason where a half would be too small
  (#4766).
