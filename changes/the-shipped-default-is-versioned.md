### Changed

- **A purlis update that changes the shipped default no longer loosens your chats on its own.**
  A forbid the update adds applies at the next decision and stays sealed, so going back to an
  older purlis does not drop it. A forbid it takes away, or loosens, waits for your Accept, which
  the policy notice says once as a change of the shipped default, with the rules that differ and
  no commit. A project rule that replaced a shipped rule the update removed stays as a plain rule
  of the project, and the window says so.
  A shipped default sealed with your accepted copy that purlis cannot read (a newer policy
  version, a parse error) is treated as a policy that does not read: chats are refused until you
  accept again, and the notice says the shipped default it was judged under does not read.
