### Changed

- **Grading a skill happens on pages inside the Skill studio** (Skill studio preview, #3688). A
  skill's Grades tab shows its latest grade as bars (with it, without it, its previous version),
  every grade with how it ended, and the grade-after-each-edit row, which says what each edit may
  spend before it is turned on. Grade opens the plan with its totals first and Start fixed at the
  foot, saying what it may spend of whose account; a plan that changed under you is held until
  you read the new one. A running grade shows its chats, its spend against its limit and its
  tokens, with Stop. The scorecard opens with how the grade ended, its totals and a row per
  trigger test, and each kept chat opens read-only from it. The scorecard and grade-chat tabs are
  gone: ones kept from an earlier launch come back as these pages.
