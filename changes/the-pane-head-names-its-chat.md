### Changed

- **A pane's head names its chat and holds its one state button.** It shows the chat's name, its
  state, its harness and workspace, and its sandbox, then one button chosen by the chat's state
  (Stop while it works, Answer while a task asks, Cancel smart close while it wraps up, Brief on
  a task), then ⋯, the bell, Split right, Split down and Close. A narrow pane drops the details
  first and keeps the bell, ⋯, Split right and Close. Right-click on the head lists the chat's
  actions, then the pane's. A task's Stop and Close now, and its Move to its own tab and Open
  beside, are in its ⋯ (#4760).
- **A tab's right-click holds only the tab's rows**: Pin, Keep open, Close other tabs and Close.
  Pressing the tab switches to it. New tab and New shell are no longer on a pane's right-click
  (#4760).
- **A chat's row in the Chats list shows its sandbox, and breathes while the chat wraps up**
  (#4760).
