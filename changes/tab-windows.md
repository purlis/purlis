### Added

- **Tear a tab off into a window of its own.** A tab's *Move to new window*, or dragging it off
  the window, puts it in a **tab window** of the same project: its strip and panes, with no
  sidebar. *Move to window ▸* moves a tab or a pane between the project's windows. Closing a tab
  window sends its tabs back to the project window and ends nothing, and tab windows come back
  after a relaunch, where they were on screen. A chat shown in a tab window is never shown
  twice: a press on it brings that window forward, and a task you are reading there is not ended
  under you, while a minimised window counts as not being looked at (#4768).
