### Added

- **The Inbox has a scope: All projects, This project or This workspace.** A switch at its top,
  the same one the Chats view has, picks whose asks are listed, each scope with its count. It
  opens on This project, and the pick is remembered for the project on this machine. This
  workspace follows the workspace in front on the strip; at the plane root it lists the asks of
  chats filed in no workspace. All projects adds the chats waiting in other windows and each
  ask of the window's other projects, read here and answered there. A scope with nothing in it
  says how many wait in the wider one, with one press to show them.

### Changed

- **An Inbox row reads in three ranks, and takes less room.** What needs you is the strongest
  line; where it is (project, workspace, persona) is quieter under it; its kind and how long it
  has waited are smallest, trailing. Each kind has a mark beside its words. A long line is cut
  to two with the whole of it in its tooltip, except the words of an ask you can allow from
  there, which are always drawn whole. Go to chat and Ignore sit side by side, and the cards
  are tighter.
- **What waits in other projects is under All projects.** The chats waiting in other windows
  and the lines for the window's other projects were listed in every Inbox; they are now listed
  in the All projects scope, and This project says how many wait there.
