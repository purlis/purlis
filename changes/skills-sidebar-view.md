### Added

- **A Skills view on the left** (Skill studio preview, #3266). With the preview on, the left
  sidebar has a Skills view under Search, on ⌘⇧X (Ctrl+Shift+X). It lists the skills of the
  workspace in the title bar by level, with what needs you on top, and at most one mark on each:
  needs review, off, left out, hidden by the router or lint. Point at a skill, or move to it with
  the keyboard, to read what it does and why it has its mark. A click opens the skill's tab, and
  its right-click menu has Use in this chat, Turn on or off for the workspace, Pin to this
  workspace (or Unpin), and Show in Explorer. Open Skill studio and New skill… are at the top, and
  Only this chat's narrows the list to the chat in front. With the preview off the view isn't
  there, and the sidebar shows Chats until the preview is back.
