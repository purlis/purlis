### Changed

- **A workspace's tab says less, in less room.** Its open chats and the ones that need you are
  one pill, the second in the needs-you colour, read as "3 chats, 1 needs you". A live workspace
  is a small dot after its name, and a pin is a small grey mark in the tab's top corner, on
  every strip. The project root's tab is a house with a tab's own padding. Every tab's title is
  one step smaller.
- **A line under the workspaces' row and between the Panel's halves.** The row of workspace
  tabs is parted from what is under it by one hairline, and the tab you are on still joins its
  content. The bottom Panel's left and right halves have the same line between them, which is
  the handle that resizes them; it was there to drag and drawn no pixels wide.
