### Changed

- **A tab says what its focused pane holds, and the pin is the tab's.** A tab wears its focused
  pane's chat icon and name, and `+N` where other panes share it; hovering it lists every pane.
  Tabs shrink to 120px and then go behind *N more*, newest activity first; a pinned tab never
  goes there, and pinned tabs show only their icons when they alone would fill half the strip.
  A narrow tab shows its × only in front or under the pointer. *Pin tab* is on the tab's menu
  and the palette, and a chat pinned before this keeps its pin on its tab (#4762).
