### Added

- Notices have a search box, a filter for each kind with its count, and groups with counts ("Clones made outside purlis · 3"). A result that names Trust this clone has Go to Trust this clone, which opens Notices on that clone's Notice with the keyboard on its button, or on Dismissed where it was dismissed. Dismissed (N) lists the notices purlis keeps dismissed until they change, and Show again brings any of them back; any other notice shows again by itself the next time it happens. Dismiss all puts away only the notices the search and the filter leave.
