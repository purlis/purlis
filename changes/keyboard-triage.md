### Added

- **Keyboard triage in Asks.** ⌘⇧I (Ctrl+Shift+I) opens Asks with the first ask's Answer, or
  its first answer that grants nothing, focused. Answering one moves the keyboard to the next,
  in another project's Asks too. ⌘⌥L puts an ask off for later, ⌘⌥G goes to its chat, ⌘⌥S
  snoozes it and ⌘⌥X stops it (Alt+Shift and the letter off a Mac). Esc, or the last ask
  answered, puts the keyboard back where it was. A press just after a row moves is still
  refused, and a high-risk ask is never moved onto (ADR 0121 decision 9, #6016).
