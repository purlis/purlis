### Security

- **A chat's screenshot paints over what you typed wherever the page draws it.** A screenshot
  the agent gets paints over what you typed or purlis filled wherever a page draws it, in text
  the page hides from its tree or draws through its style as well as in fields. On pages of the
  site where you typed, it also paints over every canvas, image and video, which purlis cannot
  read. When a page cannot be read at all, the screenshot is withheld and the agent is told why
  (#2303).
