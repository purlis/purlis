### Added

- **A chat you started yourself, or one a chat handed off to, opens its card after it closes.**
  Find it by its number in the command palette, or follow a `chat 641` link in a pane, and its
  card shows its title, when it closed, who started it and where it ran. It has no report, so
  *Reopen* is disabled, and the card says why. *Read it* opens a handoff's last screen where
  purlis kept one. purlis keeps these records on this machine, never in the committed project,
  out of every chat's reach, and deletes each one 30 days after its chat closed.
