### Changed

- **Every list is one family** (#2911). A panel's todos, personas and memories, the vault picker,
  the memory archive, Use skill, the search hits and the theme gallery are walked the same way:
  one Tab stop, the arrows stopping at the ends, typing to find a row, Enter to open or choose,
  and Shift+F10 for a row's menu. A row's pill under the pointer stands in from the panel's edge,
  a chosen row keeps its size, and the keyboard's ring is drawn inside the row. A long list in a
  dialog, a panel or Settings has a filter over it. Themes can now style a list's rows part by
  part.
