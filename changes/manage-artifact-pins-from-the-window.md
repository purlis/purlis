### Added

- **Pin to workspace from the window.** An `.html` file opened from Explorer renders privately,
  with no network and no script, and offers **Pin to workspace…** beside it and on its row. The
  dialog says the page is visible to everyone in the project, or that it stays on this machine in
  a LOCAL workspace, and lets you choose its name (suggested from its title) and an icon from
  purlis's set. A page from outside the workspace's `artifacts/` is copied into a folder of its
  own there.
- **Reorder and unpin artifacts on the activity bar.** An artifact icon's menu moves it up or
  down, or unpins it after asking, and icons can be dragged. The order and the unpin are saved
  with the project. Past eight icons, the rest are in the group's show-more, none dropped.
