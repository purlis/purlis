### Added

- **Links a program marks itself open from the pane, and ask first when their words name
  somewhere else.** A hyperlink a program prints (OSC 8) shows where it really goes when you
  hover it, and ⌘-click (Ctrl-click off a Mac) opens a web address in your browser or a file on
  this machine in an editor tab, by the same rules as a link purlis finds. When its words read
  as another address or file, purlis asks first, showing the words and where the link goes, with
  the real host in bold. *Links in this pane…* lists these links too, with their words under
  them when they name another target. purlis refuses other kinds of link and files on another
  machine, and says why (#6218).
