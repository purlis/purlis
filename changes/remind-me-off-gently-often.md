### Added

- **Remind me, in Settings › You › Attention: Off, Gently or Often.** It sets how often the ✋
  bounces again while an ask stops a chat. Gently, the default, reminds after 5, 10, 20 and 40
  minutes, then every hour; Often reminds every 5 minutes; Off never reminds, though the ✋ still
  bounces when an ask arrives. It is kept on this machine, with your other Attention settings
  (#6344).
