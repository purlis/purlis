### Added

- **A runner chat's change to the project waits for you** (#2046). When a chat on a pool's
  runner remembers something, adds a todo or edits its workspace or persona, nothing is
  written. Its tab and the Inbox show the change in words, with **Apply** and **Dismiss**, and
  the chat is told which you chose. A change too long to show whole there opens in a tab of its
  own, where you apply it. A change that carries what looks like a credential is never held.
