### Fixed

- **A pool profile's buttons are named by the words they show.** On a pool's profiles, **Sign
  in**, **Sign in to GitHub Copilot**, **Sign in to ChatGPT** and **Sign out in a terminal** had
  names that did not hold their words, so voice control could miss what you say. Each button is
  now named by its words first, then whose and where, for example `Sign in: work on pool builds`,
  as on You › Profiles (WCAG 2.5.3, Label in Name).
