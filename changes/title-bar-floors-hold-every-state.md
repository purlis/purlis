### Fixed

- **The title bar keeps its states readable under any theme** (#4000). A pinned workspace's pin
  and the row Enter would go to are checked under the pointer too. A switcher row's count is
  checked on the row colour a theme sets, and on a switcher popover a theme fills. An unavailable
  row keeps its faint look whatever the rows' colours. A workspace chip whose colours a theme set
  too faint no longer loses its hover and current-workspace fills when it draws its default back.
