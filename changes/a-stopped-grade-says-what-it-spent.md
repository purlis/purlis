### Fixed

- **A grade purlis stopped under says what its chats spent** (#3582). A grade that a crash, a
  fault or a quit interrupted used to be recorded as having spent nothing. Its record now says
  how many chats ran and the tokens and cost they reported before it stopped.
- **A grade's chat is never listed as needing you** (#3583). A chat that a grade runs is shown
  nowhere, but until the grade ended it at its first question it could briefly appear in the
  needs-you list and the away summary's count. It no longer does.
