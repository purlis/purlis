### Changed

- **A setting is one line.** Its name, its control and Reset sit side by side at one height.
  Where a group's values are kept is said once, in the group's header, instead of under every
  setting. A setting kept somewhere else, on this machine, for the team or in the workspace,
  shows a small badge; hover or focus it to read where its value comes from. A value set here
  has a small mark, and "Where it is kept" is now a "Move to…" menu (#1840).
