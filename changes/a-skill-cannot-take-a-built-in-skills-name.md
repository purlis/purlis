### Changed

- **A skill can't take a built-in skill's name** (Skill studio preview, #3680). Making, renaming, moving or sharing a skill, and accepting a chat's draft of a new one, are refused for the name of a skill purlis ships, such as `secrets` or `browser`, with "That name belongs to a built-in skill; pick another, or make a copy." The window and `purlis skill` refuse it alike. Restoring a deleted skill, installing one from a source, and an update that brings along a skill under such a name are refused too. A skill already in the project under such a name stays where it is, and no chat gets it.
