### Fixed

- **The theme check reads every part's words on the fill under them.** A popover's key, a tab's
  count, a title-bar crumb and a switcher's row are each measured on their own pill or surface,
  in the colour the window draws them in, so a theme can no longer pass by setting a part's
  words to the colour of its own cap. A tab's ×, the title bar's ⌄ and a row's pin are held as
  marks, at 3:1 (#3099).
