### Changed

- **A chat's browser asks you once, not at every tool.** In a Claude Code chat, the browser
  tools no longer stop on Claude Code's own permission prompt. Your answer to the chat's browser
  Ask (this project's sites, also the open web, or Keep off) is what lets them run, as your
  answer to a dispatch is for a dispatch. Your own `ask` or `deny` rules for these tools still
  win, and a browser still reaches only what you allowed (#2245).
