### Changed

- **A press on a chat goes to the pane you used last, in whichever of the project's windows.**
  With a tab window open, a press on a chat that no window shows lands in the most recently
  used pane across the project window and its tab windows, and that window comes forward. A
  minimised window is passed over. With one window nothing changes: the chat lands in the
  focused pane (story 97, #5108).
