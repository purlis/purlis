### Changed

- **`purlis theme check` reads a notice card where it is drawn** (#4210). A card's title and mark
  are measured on the ground each card stands on (news on the overlay, trouble and attention on
  their own), and a news card's mark is held in its success colour at 3:1. A new finding,
  `notice-way-not-apart`, names a theme whose notice card's secondary button would stand too
  close to the card to read as a button.
