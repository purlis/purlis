### Added

- **The Browsers grid.** *Open browsers*, in the palette and on the Chats view's heading while a
  project has a browser, opens a tab of every browser as a live tile, about a frame a second: a
  chat's, by its chat, and your own. Its switch is the Inbox's: this project (the default), the
  workspace whose strip the tab is on, or every project the window holds, each with its count.
  Pressing a tile opens that browser's view in its own project (#2027).
