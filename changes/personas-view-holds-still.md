### Changed

- **The Personas view keeps its rows still under you** (#3566). A persona that worked in the
  focused workspace stays under In this workspace after its chat closes, with or without a
  session record, for as long as the window holds the project. While the pointer is over the
  list or the keyboard is in it, no persona changes group or place. The move happens once both
  have left, as in the Chats list.
