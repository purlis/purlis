### Added

- **A pinned page can offer you an action, and only you do it.** An approved artifact page can
  offer one of its workspace's todos to open, mark done, reopen, forget or delete. purlis shows
  the offer as a button on the artifact's own bar, labelled the way the palette labels it, and
  nothing happens until you press it. Forget and delete ask first. purlis refuses any other
  action a page offers, and says why.
