### Changed

- **Every menu, context menu and popover is one family** (#2909). A tab's menu, a row's
  right-click menu, a terminal's, a tab's task chip, the strip's show-more, a secret's and a
  setting's *Move to…* are drawn and walked the same way: under what opened them, lined up with
  it and never against the window's edge, closed by a click outside or by Escape, the arrows
  stopping at their ends, a row that cannot be undone in the danger colour, the key that does the
  same at a row's end. A row's card and a refused name share their surface. Themes can now style
  a menu's rows and a popover's surface part by part.
