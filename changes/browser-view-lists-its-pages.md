- **A browser's view says when its chat opened several pages, and you can watch another.**
  _Page 2 of 3_ follows the page's title; its menu lists each page by its title and site, as the
  chat's `browser_pages` lists them, with the one shown ticked. Picking another takes the browser
  over, as _Back_ does, so it never acts for the agent, and _Done_ gives the agent its own page
  back. A title holding a value purlis filled is scrubbed, and an address holding one shows no
