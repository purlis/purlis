### Added

- **Known gaps are declared, and the chat still starts.** Where a rule in force is one a harness cannot
  enforce on this system, such as a hook Codex does not have, Claude Code's reads, a sandbox lifted
  for one chat or the missing sandbox on Windows, purlis says so in Settings (*Sandbox › Known
  gaps*), in `purlis doctor`, in the chat's start event and on its pane, for a chat started from the
  window as for one a host starts. A project that wants such a start refused writes a forbid on
  `start` over `context.gaps` and enforces the family `trust`; under `log` the start goes ahead
  and says once that the rule would have refused it, and an `@ask` forbid on `start` refuses
  until asking at a start is wired. The shipped default has no such rule (M75-37, #7046).
