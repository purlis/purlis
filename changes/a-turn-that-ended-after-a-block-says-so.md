### Added

- **Updates › Mine lists each chat's finished turn and failed chat.** A turn that ended while
  something purlis, the sandbox, a policy or a project setting blocked was still unresolved adds
  "· 1 blocked this turn" to its row. A later success on the same target, or an Allow, resolves
  the block. A blocked turn is still a finished turn, never a failure, and identical rows from
  several chats are grouped into one. The summary after Quiet counts them as "stopped after a
  block".
