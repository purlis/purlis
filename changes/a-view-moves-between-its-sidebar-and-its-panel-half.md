### Added

- **Move a view between its sidebar and its half of the Panel.** Right-click a view's icon, or
  press Shift+F10 on it, and choose "Move to panel" or "Move to sidebar": the view goes to the
  other place on its own side and opens there. Moves never cross to the other side, and the
  Inbox stays pinned to the right sidebar. An extension's panel moves like any view on the
  right. Where each view lives is kept for each project, and _Use the default layout_ puts
  every view back (spec #1736, #1740).
