### Changed

- **A skill only on this machine keeps its grades across a rename and a move** (#4018). Git keeps
  no history of a local skill, so this machine now keeps the names and levels it had, and its
  Grades tab and each grade's scorecard read the grades recorded under them. This holds for a
  rename, a move into or out of the local skills, and a share. The Rename and Move dialogs say its
  grades move with it, and that its History starts again.
