### Added

- **A pinned artifact opens in a tab.** A workspace's pinned pages (`purlis artifact pin`) have an
  icon each on the left activity bar, in a group of their own below purlis's icons. Pressing one
  opens the page in a private tab, or brings its tab forward. The page runs isolated from the
  window, with no network and no script, under a bar purlis draws that names who pinned it. A tab
  put back when purlis starts runs nothing until you press Open. In the page, Esc brings the
  keyboard back to purlis and F6 moves on.
