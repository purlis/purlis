### Changed

- **A notice card's mark and title are theme parts, and its secondary ways look like buttons**
  (#3478). Themes reach a card's mark as `notice.mark` (its colour) and its title as
  `notice.title` (colour, size and weight). The theme check reads the title's words on the card's
  ground. On a notice card, a secondary button such as "Add a vision" now has a visible fill in
  Purlis dark and light, where it used to read as plain words.
