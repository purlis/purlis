### Added

- **A chat's browser on its tab and in the Chats list.** Each browser a chat drives is drawn
  live, as a small picture, beside the chat's tab, and listed under the chat in the Chats list;
  either opens it in a tab of its own, which names the device it runs on above the page.
  Closing that tab closes only the view: the browser ends with its chat. Settings › You ›
  Chats list › *Open a chat's browser beside it* opens each new one beside its chat (#2024).
