### Changed

- **A chat's theme specimen draws any theme the window could draw there.** `theme_preview` names
  a theme folder of the chat's workspace, the project or your own by its id, an extension's
  theme, or `draft:<folder>`, the chat's own theme draft, with the theme's option values, and
  draws it side by side with another, such as a draft beside the theme as kept. The specimen is
  tinted with the workspace's colour and drawn in the theme's own fonts, and its terminal in the
  theme's terminal font. Settings › Theme's cards are pictured in their fonts too (#2536).
