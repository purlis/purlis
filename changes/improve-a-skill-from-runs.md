### Added

- **Draft a skill's outcome evals with a chat, and improve a skill from its runs** (#2780). A
  skill's Grades tab offers Ask a chat to write outcome evals, and its More menu offers Improve
  from runs…. Each opens a chat with its request typed and not sent. The chat reads the skill, and
  for Improve its recent runs and grades, and what it writes comes back as a draft for you to accept
  or discard: nothing changes in the skill until you accept it.
