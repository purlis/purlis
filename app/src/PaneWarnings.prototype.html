State


    
Rules

      
    
        
  1. Nothing sits between a tab and its pane. A pane's warning lives inside the pane.
    2. 
        
  2. The top 8px of a pane is always the terminal's own fill, so the chosen tab and its flares join the terminal, not a warning.
    3. 
        
  3. Nothing draws over the terminal. Chips and warnings each take a row of their own.
    4. 
        
  4. The terminal is pinned to its bottom. Showing or dismissing a warning grows or shrinks it from the top; the last line never moves.
    5. 
        
  5. At most two warnings show. The rest wait behind "+N more", which has its own row and a reserved width.
    6. 
        
  6. The warnings never take more than about half the pane; past that they scroll inside their band.
    7. 
        
  7. A warning is a wash (.12): wash.warning, or danger.wash for trouble. No edge, no hairline, no amber box.
    8. 
        
  8. Corners: 7 controls, 8 tabs and pills, 10 warnings, 12 panels and panes.
    9. 
        
  9. A consent warning has no Dismiss. Allow stands apart from Keep blocked, carries its open-lock mark, and neither is drawn as primary.
    10. 
        
  10. Text tiers: the warning's words primary, its detail quiet, marks faint.
    11. 
        
  11. Focus is a 2px lavender ring: inset inside a strip or a pane, outside a control.
    12. 
        
  12. The separator's hover is quiet (strong border tier, 2px), never lavender (#3143).
    13. 
        
  13. A long warning wraps in place and breaks a path anywhere. Its ways out drop under the words when they do not fit.
    14. 
        
  14. Esc on a focused warning dismisses it; focus moves to the next warning, or to the terminal.
    15. 
        
  15. No state change moves a neighbour: chips, pill, counts and chevrons hold fixed slots.
